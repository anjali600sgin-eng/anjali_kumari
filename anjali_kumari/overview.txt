PLIXORA
Online Gaming Website - Overview
==============================================================

Plixora is a free online gaming website where people can jump
straight into browser games. No downloads, no installs, and no
sign up. You open the page, pick a game, and you are playing
within seconds, whether you are on a phone, a tablet, or a laptop.

The idea is simple. Most casual games today hide behind app
stores, accounts, or cluttered, ad heavy pages. Plixora pulls
instantly playable games into one clean, fast place that is easy
to browse and safe for all ages.

It is a full product, not just a nice looking front page. There
is the website people visit, a backend with APIs, a database that
holds the games, and an admin panel so the team can add and update
games without touching any code. Signing in stays optional, but if
you do make an account, your favourites and recently played games
follow you from one device to the next.

A few quick facts:
- Name: Plixora
- Tagline: Play Instantly. Anywhere. Free.
- What it is: an instant play games portal
- Works on: desktop, tablet, and mobile
- Built with: PERN stack (PostgreSQL, Express, React, Node.js)
- Brand colours: Plixora Purple #7C5CFF with Teal #00D1B2

Who it is for: casual players who want a quick break, students
between classes, mobile users who would rather not install an app,
and parents looking for something safe for their kids. Behind the
scenes, admins keep the catalogue fresh.

What success looks like: the homepage loads in under 2.5 seconds,
you are in a game in under 5, and the site feels just as good on a
phone as on a desktop.

In scope: the website, the API and database behind it, search,
categories, favourites, recently played, ratings, optional
accounts, and the admin panel. Out of scope: building the games
themselves, payments, native apps, and ads.
